The Catalog

Six Disciplines. One Standard.

Every class is capped at 16 athletes and led by a certified coach. First-timers get a movement screen before their first session.

Strength

Forge Strength

The barbell program that built our reputation. Squat, bench, deadlift and press on a 12-week progressive block, with video-reviewed technique every session. Four scaling levels mean day-one beginners train safely next to seasoned lifters.

60 minMedium intensityMon / Wed / Fri
Conditioning

Ignite HIIT

45 minutes of sled pushes, rope slams, kettlebell complexes and assault-bike intervals. Heart-rate zones are displayed live so you train in the right gear — red for athletes, orange for everyone else.

45 minHigh intensityTue / Thu / Sat
Combat

Iron Boxing

Six rounds on the pads, three on the heavy bag, and a conditioning finisher. Footwork, combinations and defence taught from zero — wraps and gloves provided for trial members.

60 minHigh intensityMon / Thu / Sat
Engine

Engine Room

Row, ski and ride intervals built around your own wattage zones. The lowest-impact way to build a monster aerobic base — ideal for runners, cyclists and anyone rebuilding fitness.

45 minMedium intensityTue / Fri / Sun
Skill

Olympic Lifting Club

Snatch and clean & jerk taught the patient way: positions first, load later. Video analysis every week and a quarterly in-house meet to test your numbers.

75 minMedium intensityWed / Sat
Recovery

Mobility Lab

Guided stretching, joint-prep flows and breathwork in the recovery studio. The session your future self will thank you for — free for Unlimited and Elite members.

40 minLow intensityDaily
