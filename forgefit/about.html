Since 2017

We Got Tired of Gyms That Don't Coach

ForgeFit started in 2017 as a two-car garage with one barbell, a whiteboard, and a stubborn belief: ordinary people get extraordinary results when someone actually coaches them.

Nine years later we coach over 2,400 members across three training zones — but the rulebook hasn't changed. Every session is led by a certified coach. Every member trains on a program, not a whim. And nobody — beginner or competitor — trains alone.

What We Stand For

The ForgeFit Code

Coach everything

If it's on our floor, it's coached. Technique is a skill and skills need eyes on them.

Track everything

Lifts, times, benchmarks — if it isn't measured it isn't managed. Re-testing every 12 weeks.

Scale everything

Every workout has four levels. The right version of hard beats the wrong version of harder.

Recover deliberately

Training breaks you down; recovery builds you up. We program both with equal seriousness.
